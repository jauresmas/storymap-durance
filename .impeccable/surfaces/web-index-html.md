# Surface brief: StoryMap « 50 ans au fil de la Durance » (web/index.html)

Scope: the whole published StoryMap page. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from
the first viewport). Audience: geomatics/water recruiters judging the author's craft in a
few minutes, desktop first, phone must hold. Job: understand how a valley syndicate became
the Durance's basin authority, and see the author's method. Constraints: independent work,
no SMAVD logo; every fact sourced; static site, MapLibre GL JS, self-hosted assets.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this harness).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The story is a 50-year archive dossier opened on the reader's desk: each chapter is a numbered pièce with date and source cote, and the live map is the "plan annexé" that changes with each pièce. Refuses the category default: a white sidebar of text cards beside a stock basemap.

OWN-WORLD: Turquoise pressboard chemise owning whole regions (cover, intercalaire tabs); white typed sheets; violet rubber-stamp ink for dates and cotes; black typewriter face only for measured data and cotes; Archivo in condensed widths as Dymo-label and heading voice, text width for reading. The map is a drawn plan: paper ground, IGN estompage relief, water blues, one thematic subject at full strength, the rest receding; a cartouche with legend, scale and live readout.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor sees a real river inside a real dossier. Scrolling opens the folder and walks nine pièces, 1955 to 2026 (amended after the first review: the EDF canal, the 1950/today comparison and the closing pièce each earned a sheet); each claim shows its reference. The dossier closes on a signed colophon exposing the method: QGIS, PyQGIS scripts, MapLibre.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed turquoise chemise. A flat black Dymo tape "DURANCE" reads up the folder's spine (not above the title). Left 40%: typed title sticker "50 ans au fil de la Durance", subtitle, cote line with the violet stamp "1976 – 2026" at its right end. Right 60%: a rectangular window die-cut in the cover showing the live map, the river drawing itself downstream from Serre-Ponçon to the Rhône. Intercalaire year tabs on the cover's right edge. Primary action "Ouvrir le dossier" bottom-left.

FORM: Administrative archive dossier, candidate 7 of my ordered list (1 longitudinal profile, 2 topographic sheet, 3 relief atlas, 4 EDF engineering plans, 5 limnigraph chart, 6 flood-mark plaques, 7 archive dossier); seed key f719d8c3.
- Raise from the Fletcher poster: one idea per plate; each chapter's map highlights a single subject, everything else recedes.
- Raise from the type specimen: a live readout in the cartouche (year, coordinates, zoom) follows the camera.
- Raise from the split-flap board: year and pièce number change by per-character cascade in fixed cells; layout never jumps.
- Raise from the glaze shelf: flow and gravity; the river draws itself downstream when the dossier opens.
- Raise from the sampler: the dossier closes on a signed, dated colophon (author, method, sources).
- Raise from the sneaker-box wall: every pièce carries the same label grid: N°, date, reference (the founding act or dataset, e.g. "loi n° 55-6"), sources.
- Materials are flat (review fix): no simulated grain, ink texture, emboss or punched holes; stamps are flat violet ink with a double border.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
